CardFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026 · Package: com.shubhamanap.cardflow

Privacy · Terms of Service

CardFlow (“we”, “the app”, “us”) is a business-card and contact QR scanner for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed, why, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the app, you acknowledge this policy.

1. Who we are

CardFlow is published on Google Play under package com.shubhamanap.cardflow. Privacy questions: email shubhamanap01@gmail.com.

2. Data on your device

Contacts, groups, profile details you create, app settings, usage limits related to scanning, purchase status needed for Pro features, and optional card photos are stored on your device. Your contact list is not uploaded to a CardFlow cloud address book.

3. Account data

If you create an account (email or Google Sign-In), we store your email, optional display name, plan (free or Pro), and limited usage counters so we can manage your account and entitlements. Google Sign-In is provided by Google; we receive your email and basic name when you authorize the app.

4. Scanning

When you scan a business card, text may be read on your device and, for some plans or when needed for accuracy, a card image may be sent securely over the internet to our scanning service and trusted processing partners. When you scan a contact QR code, the code is read on your device and is not uploaded for OCR. Do not scan cards or codes you are not allowed to process. We do not run a public gallery of your scans. Short-lived technical logs may be used to prevent abuse.

5. Device and advertising identifiers

A device or app identifier may be used to limit abuse of scanning features. When ads are shown, advertising identifiers may be used by our advertising partner under their policies.

6. Advertising

The free plan may show ads (including banner, full-screen, and optional rewarded ads). Our advertising partner may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, and ad interaction data under their own privacy policy. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in system settings. Pro users do not see CardFlow ads.

7. Purchases

Optional Pro upgrades are sold through Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment card details. Play may share purchase status with the app so entitlements can be applied and restored. Plan status may also be stored with your account.

8. Exports and backups you create

If you export, share, or back up data, files leave the app through your device’s share or file tools under your control. We do not receive those files unless you choose to send them to us.

9. Purposes

We process data to provide scanning and contacts features, authenticate you, apply free-plan limits, show ads where applicable, unlock Pro features, prevent abuse, and comply with law. Where consent is required, we rely on that consent.

10. Retention

On-device data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app. Account data is kept while your account exists. After you delete your account, we remove associated account records, subject to backups or legal holds where required.

11. Your choices and rights

In the app you can open this Privacy Policy and the Terms, clear local data, sign out, delete your account (Settings), or contact support at shubhamanap01@gmail.com. Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port personal data, and to complain to a supervisory authority.

12. Children

CardFlow is intended for business and professional use and is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Contact us if you believe a child has created an account.

13. International transfers

Service providers that help us run CardFlow (for example authentication, hosting, scanning, ads, and app distribution) may process data in the United States or other countries under their safeguards. By using the app you understand processing may occur outside your country.

14. Security

We use industry-standard protections such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure storage of sign-in sessions on your device where available. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

15. Service providers

We use trusted third parties for account authentication, cloud hosting, scanning assistance, advertising, and Google Play distribution and billing. Their privacy policies apply to their services.

16. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy where permitted by law.
